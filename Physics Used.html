Fields of physics

  1. General Relativity (GTR) Schwarzschild radius Rs = 2GM/c², rotating (Kerr) solution and frame-dragging, event horizon, the Package Formula Rs = N × 8.27 km.
  2. Astrophysics Stellar fusion up to iron, supernovae, neutron stars, the TOV limit, black holes, accretion disks, X-ray binaries (Cygnus X-1, GRO J1655-40, GRS 1915+105), magnetars, Sagittarius A*.
  3. Nuclear Physics Hydrogen-to-helium fusion and mass defect (about 0.7%), beta decay, electron capture, magic numbers, He-4 and C-12 as analogues.
  4. Standard Model Quarks (up, down), leptons, gluons, W± and Z⁰ bosons, photon, Higgs boson, the four fundamental forces.
  5. Quantum Chromodynamics The strong force, binding energy (about 99% of nucleon mass), quark confinement and deconfinement, quark-gluon plasma.
  6. Electroweak Theory and the Higgs Mechanism Higgs field (246 GeV), mass from the Higgs (about 1%), reverse phase transition, symmetry restoration (v → 0), melting of W and Z.
  7. Quantum Mechanics Tunneling through the Coulomb barrier, Pauli exclusion principle, uncertainty principle, degeneracy pressure, the quantized “package”.
  8. Thermodynamics and Statistical Physics Phase transitions, equation of state P = 1/3 u, radiation pressure, thermal energy budget (OpenQuestion).
  9. Electrodynamics Coulomb repulsion, magnetic field energy B²/2μ₀, finite-core dynamo with a conductive shell, magnetar fields.
  10. Relativistic Mechanics Relativistic and ultra-relativistic gas, particles moving at c, E = mc², barrier energy 5.01 × 10⁴⁷ J.
  11. Classical Mechanics Rotation, angular momentum, gravity–rotation balance, spin parameter a*.
  12. Cosmology The early Universe about 10⁻¹² s after the Big Bang, electroweak epoch, composition of the Milky Way (74% hydrogen, 24% helium).
  13. Physical Mathematics Dimensionless parameter η, scaling, unit conversion (GeV → J), the linear Package Formula, hexagonal geometry.
  14. Chemistry and Emergence Atoms as the foundation of chemistry, then molecules, biology, life – the window of life.
  15. Physics of Time (hypothesis) Time as a sequence, gravitational slowing of time, the frequency of time, formula 5+1+5.

How they are combined

  1. Higgs field gives quarks about 1% of their mass (Higgs mechanism)
  2. Strong force binds them and generates the other 99% (QCD)
  3. Weak force turns the neutron into a proton and an electron (nuclear physics)
  4. Electromagnetism builds atoms, the foundation of chemistry (electrodynamics, quantum mechanics)
  5. Chemistry → biology → life in the window of life, held by stellar fusion (astrophysics, thermodynamics)
  6. Fuel runs out and gravity, the weakest force, reverses the sequence (general relativity)
  7. Finite core with W, Z and Higgs layers, about 15.8 m (electroweak theory, relativistic mechanics)
  8. Black hole built from packages of 2.8 M☉ (Package Formula, cosmology)
